# Code of conduct / Verhaltenskodex

Friends-SNCMS welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Treat contributors, users and maintainers with respect.

- Critique code and decisions with evidence; do not attack people or pressure volunteers.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing or publication of private communications without permission.
- No spam, covert promotion, malicious code or intentionally misleading test/security claims.
- Respect scope decisions and reviews. Explain disagreement calmly and accept a final maintainer decision without repeated disruption.
- Help newcomers with concrete, constructive feedback. Mistakes are an opportunity to correct the work.

These rules apply to project issues, PRs, discussions, wiki edits and other official project spaces. Maintainers may remove content, warn contributors, restrict interaction or revoke access according to severity. Where safe, they should state the reason and avoid exposing private reports.

For safety concerns, use the private project reporting channel once it has been configured. A dedicated contact address is not yet established. Do not post private details publicly or use an issue to expose a vulnerability; see `SECURITY.md`. The project lead, Andreas Holzer @WebCrew, is responsible for appointing moderators and handling escalations.

Deutsch: Sachlich, respektvoll und nachvollziehbar zusammenarbeiten. Keine persönlichen Angriffe, Diskriminierung, Belästigung, privaten Daten, Spam oder absichtlich irreführenden Angaben. Maintainer dürfen Inhalte moderieren und Zugriffe einschränken. Ein privater Meldeweg muss bei der Repository-Einrichtung noch festgelegt werden.
